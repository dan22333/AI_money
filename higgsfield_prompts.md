# Higgsfield Prompt Pack — Jenny Carter

Ready-to-paste prompts to generate Jenny's content with a **consistent look**.
Workflow: 1) generate a locked reference portrait → 2) reuse it as the character reference for
every scene → 3) turn the best stills into short videos with the image-to-video prompts.

> Keep everything tasteful and within Instagram / Fanvue platform rules.
> Only swap Jenny into footage you own or have licensed — never a real person's video.

---

## STEP 1 — Lock the look (base reference portrait)

Generate this first, pick the best result, and set it as the **character reference** for everything else.

```
Photorealistic portrait of a 25-year-old woman named Jenny: natural blonde hair in a loose
ponytail, athletic toned fitness build, warm friendly smile, light golden tan, natural glam
makeup, bright blue-green eyes. Wearing a fitted white tank top. Soft natural daylight, shallow
depth of field, 85mm lens look, clean bright Miami aesthetic. Ultra-realistic skin texture,
high detail, color-accurate. A thin fabric scrunchie on her left wrist.
```

**Consistency rules for every later prompt:** keep face, hair color, body type, and the
left-wrist scrunchie identical. Only change scene, outfit, pose, lighting, and camera.

## Posting use (quick guide)
- **Instagram (free):** brighter, teaser energy — beach run, tennis, gym, with-dog. Pull people in.
- **Fanvue (paid):** more personal/exclusive — apartment, glam, beach day. Reward subscribers.
- Match the mood to the story beat (grit/discipline vs. warm/connection) from the persona bible.
